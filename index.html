THE BEGINNING

BEFORE THE MEMES, THERE WAS TAKO.

Long before Kabosu became an internet icon, the story of the Shiba Inu had already begun.

In 1932, a red Shiba named Tako became the first of the breed officially registered by NIPPO — setting the foundation for one of Japan’s most iconic native dogs.

TAKO represents that origin story — a tribute to the history, character and spirit that came before the meme era. Now that story lives on-chain.
